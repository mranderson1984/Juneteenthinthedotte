— About Us

Juneteenth in the Dotte

"The longest-running African-American holiday — America's Second Independence Day."

Juneteenth in the Dotte exists to promote Wyandotte County's Annual Juneteenth Parade. Our committee recognizes this holiday as a celebration of freedom, resilience, and community — honored every year on the third Saturday in June.

Participation

Providing opportunities for local organizations and individuals of all backgrounds to participate and compete in prize categories.

Community

Creating a greater sense of an empowered and educated community throughout the Wyandotte County area.

Culture

Uniquely contributing to the quality, vitality, and cultural richness of the Wyandotte County community.
